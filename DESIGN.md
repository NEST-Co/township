# Fernhollow Township (concept)

Fictional township. Not a real government. Tone: plain, calm, helpful. Residents should find a form, a meeting or a phone number in two clicks.

## Look (redesigned Oct 28, 2026)
Civic and structured. Pine green with brass accents on warm paper. A panoramic illustration of the township hall and fire station opens the home page, with "what do you need to do?" tiles overlapping it. Notices and events use calendar-style date badges. Interior pages open with a green page band and breadcrumb.

## Tokens
- Brand: pine #1B4D3E, deep #123428, brass #E3C26A (decorative and on dark only), paper #FBF9F3. Text #14201B. Muted #44504A. Focus #B3470A.
- Fonts: system stacks only (Avenir Next / Segoe UI), so nothing loads and nothing shifts.
- Images: hand-built SVG in `public/img/` (hero, station, meeting). Icons are inline SVG in `Icon.astro`.

## Pages
Home (top tasks, next meeting, notices, events, Fire & EMS feature) · Services · Fire & EMS · Meetings · Documents (filterable) · Notices · About · Contact · FAQ · Accessibility statement.

## Demonstrates
Top-task homepage, document archive with file type and size, meeting calendar from data, accessibility statement with measured scores (added only after axe/Lighthouse runs), no PDF-only content for key info.
